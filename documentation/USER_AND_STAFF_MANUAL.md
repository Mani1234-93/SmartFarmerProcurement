# User, Staff & Administrator Operational Manual

## 1. Farmer Journey

```text
Language Choice -> Registration/Login -> Select Centre & Crop -> Choose Slot -> Get Token & QR Code -> Live Queue Tracking -> Counter Verification & Weighing -> Digital Receipt & DBT Payment
```

### A. Language Selection & Registration
- Open the app and choose your preferred language: **English**, **हिंदी (Hindi)**, or **छत्तीसगढ़ी (Chhattisgarhi)**.
- Enter your 10-digit mobile number and PIN to log in, or complete the 1-minute registration form with your Kisan Card number, village, and bank details.

### B. Booking a Procurement Slot
1. On the dashboard, tap **Book Slot**.
2. Select your nearest procurement centre from the verified directory.
3. Select your produce (e.g. *Paddy Common*, *Paddy Grade A*, *Wheat*).
4. Enter your estimated produce quantity in Quintals.
5. Pick an available hourly slot (e.g. `09:00 AM - 10:00 AM`).
6. Tap **Confirm Booking** to generate your unique **Booking ID** (e.g. `BK202608260012`), **Token Number** (e.g. `A-124`), and **Verification QR Code**.

### C. Live Queue Tracking & Arriving at Centre
- Tap **Queue Status** on your dashboard.
- Check **Now Serving**, **People Ahead**, and **Estimated Wait Time**.
- When 5 or fewer farmers are ahead of you, proceed to the centre.

### D. Digital Receipts & Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
- Once weighing and quality inspection are completed by staff, your receipt is immediately generated.
- View and share the official PDF receipt via WhatsApp or SMS.
- Payment is transferred directly to your registered bank account within 24–48 hours.

## 2. Procurement Staff Operations

### A. Managing Daily Queue
1. Log in using your assigned Employee credentials.
2. The **Staff Portal** displays today's live queue.
3. Tap **Call Next Farmer** to announce the next in line.

### B. Farmer Verification (QR Scanning)
1. Tap **Scan Farmer QR** and scan the farmer's mobile screen (or type the Token / Booking ID).
2. The farmer's verified profile, crop, and expected quantity will automatically load.

### C. Produce Intake, Weighing & Grading
1. Enter the **Gross Weight** from the weighbridge and **Tare Weight** of the vehicle.
2. Enter the measured **Moisture Percentage** (e.g. $14\%$).
3. Select the quality grade (**Grade A**, **Grade B**, or **Grade C**).
4. The system calculates the Net Weight, deductions, and total payable amount.
5. Tap **Complete Procurement & Issue Receipt** to conclude the transaction.

## 3. Administrator Portal

### A. Dashboard Metrics
- Monitor real-time statewide intake, daily bookings, queues, and disbursed payment amounts.

### B. Managing Capacity & Prices
- **Centres**: Set daily intake caps (e.g. 1,500 Quintals/day) and operational hours.
- **Crops & MSP**: Update official support prices (₹/Quintal) and moisture limits per season.
- **Reports**: Export consolidated CSV records for State Civil Supplies and bank reconciliations.
